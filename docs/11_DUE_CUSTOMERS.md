## 📌 Purpose & Key Features

The **Due Customers** module manages customer credit accounts, pending tabs, and balance settlements for regular guests and corporate clients.

---

## 💳 Settlement & Credit Ledger
- **Credit Inflow**: Placing a POS bill with payment method `Due` logs the unpaid balance to the customer's phone profile.
- **Partial & Full Settlement**: Record payments when the customer returns to clear their balance.
- **Total Outstanding Summary**: Total credit owed across all customers displayed at a glance.
