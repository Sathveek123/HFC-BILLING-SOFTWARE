## 📌 Purpose & Key Features

The **Expenses Tracker** logs non-billing operational costs (Gas Cylinder refills, Utility bills, Packaging supplies, Staff food, Equipment maintenance) to calculate true net profitability.

---

## 📊 Expense Categorization
- **Raw Materials**: Vendor ingredient purchases.
- **Utilities**: Commercial gas cylinder, electricity, water.
- **Supplies**: Takeaway containers, paper bags, tissue boxes.
- **Maintenance**: Cleaning supplies, kitchen repair.
- **Staff Expenses**: Staff meals, travel allowances.
