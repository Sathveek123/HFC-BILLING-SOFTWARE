## 📌 Purpose & Key Features

The **Items & Stock** section enables management of dish availability and daily item inventory counts to prevent waiters from billing sold-out items.

---

## ⚡ Capabilities
- **Availability Toggle**: Switch items `In Stock` / `Out of Stock` instantly.
- **Stock Count Adjustment**: Edit daily portion counts (e.g. 50 portions of Chicken Biryani available).
- **POS Warning Sync**: Items marked out of stock or zero quantity are automatically disabled on the POS billing screen.
